WHO WE ARE

Building Stronger, Healthier Communities

Jnanatarangini Foundation is a registered Section 8 Non-Profit Organization dedicated to creating sustainable, positive social impact within rural and underserved communities.

Through community participation, partnerships and sustainable initiatives, the Foundation works to uplift vulnerable individuals and households while promoting social equity and collective advancement.
